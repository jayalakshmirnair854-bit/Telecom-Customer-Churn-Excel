# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing telecom customer churn using Microsoft Excel. The analysis helps identify customer churn patterns based on region, subscription plan, age group, tenure, and satisfaction score.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn.
- Identify churn patterns across different regions.
- Compare churn rates by subscription plan.
- Analyze churn based on customer age groups.
- Understand the relationship between tenure and churn.
- Explore how customer satisfaction affects churn.

## 📂 Dataset Information

- *Dataset:* Telecom Subscription Customer Churn Dataset
- *Source:* Kaggle
- *Original Records:* 50,200
- *Records After Cleaning:* 50,000
- *Number of Columns:* 18

## 💡 Key Insights

- Customers aged 40–49 have the highest age-group churn rate (10.54%).
- South region has the highest churn rate (10.48%).
- New customers show a higher churn rate (14.09%).
- Long-term customers have a lower churn rate (2.16%).
- Customers with satisfaction score 1 have the highest churn rate (30.72%).
- Customers with satisfaction score 5 have the lowest churn rate (2.26%).

## 🎯 Business Impact

- Identify customers at risk of leaving.
- Improve customer retention strategies.
- Understand customer satisfaction patterns.
- Support better subscription plan decisions.
- Reduce customer churn through data-driven insights.
